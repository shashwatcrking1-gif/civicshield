THE PROBLEM

Compound Urban Failure

When Chennai experienced historic monsoons, the tragedy was not solely the rainfall—it was the subsequent chain of events: clogged stormwater canals caused arterial road flooding, which halted city buses, gridlocked ambulances, and stranded critical patients away from hospitals.

OUR VISION

Anticipatory Urban Resilience

To transform cities into self-anticipating organisms where infrastructure disruptions are predicted hours in advance, allowing human authorities to deploy precision interventions that sever the disaster cascade at its weakest link.

Key System Objectives

Bridge Siloed Departments: Unify Greater Chennai Corporation, traffic police, 108 ambulance dispatch, and hospital administration under one shared operational picture.
Deterministic Risk Propagation: Compute multi-tier cascade probabilities with complete mathematical transparency instead of unpredictable AI black boxes.
Actionable Break Points: Guide field commanders directly to the highest-leverage intervention point to maximize disaster mitigation efficiency.
Strict Human Authority Governance: Ensure that artificial intelligence only advises and recommends; execution decisions remain with verified authorities.
CURRENT SCOPE & LIMITATIONS

System Assumptions

  • The current testbed uses simulated data calibrated to Chennai's topography (Velachery, Adyar, T. Nagar, Guindy).
  • Sensor latency and network interruptions are simulated via configurable data source health statuses.
  • Hydraulic flood modeling is approximated via empirical propagation weights.
FUTURE HORIZONS

Roadmap & Evolution

  • Direct integration with live SCADA telemetry on Greater Chennai Corporation sluice gates.
  • Automated SMS and WhatsApp citizen evacuation advisories via government alert gateways.
  • Expansion of the cascade graph to include electrical sub-station failure and drinking water contamination.
