01

T=0m: Cloudburst Detected (115 mm/hr)

IMD radar registers sudden heavy precipitation over Velachery basin. CivicShield ingests the signal and calculates a 92% Rain probability.

02

T+15m: Drainage Capacity Exceeded

Factoring in the low-lying topography and 35% effective drain clearance, the engine flags an 81% Flood probability with waterlogging on Velachery Main Road.

03

T+30m: Underpass Blockage & Gridlock Forecast

Before vehicles arrive at the flooded junction, CivicShield forecasts a 74% Road Blockage and 69% peak traffic spillover to 100 Feet Road.

04

T+45m: Pre-Emptive Ambulance Rerouting

108 Emergency dispatch is automatically alerted that ambulances headed for Velachery Govt. Hospital face a 58% delay risk. A green corridor via Inner Ring Road is initiated.
